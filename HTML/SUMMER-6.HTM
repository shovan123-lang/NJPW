Match observations: 

Hakata Star Lanes
Fukuoka, Japan
July 18, 1991
Attendance: 2,000
Average ticket: $25
Gate: $50,000
Merchandise $3,200

Summer Crush '91 reached Fukuoka for night seven, and once again New Japan
had to reshuffle its main event. Scott Norton, scheduled to team with Keiji
Mutoh and Masahiro Chono, missed his connection to Fukuoka and never
reached the building. Kensuke Sasaki took his place.

Hiroyoshi Tenzan opened the show in style, lifting Jeff Gaylord high and
driving him down with the Mountain Bomb at 8:58. Four days after being
thrown into the Hiroshima main event, the young man looked every bit like
he belongs there.

El Samurai beat Norio Honaga with the inverted swinging DDT in just over
ten minutes, his second straight win with the move and his second step
toward Jushin Liger and the IWGP Junior title at Sumo Hall.

Masashi Aoyagi and Tarzan Goto picked up where Abashiri left off, but the
rematch ended in chaos at 8:55 when Goto grabbed a chair and uncontrolably 
swung the chair around going after Aoyagi! Tony Halme ran down and tried
to make it 2-1 against Aoyagi, but out came Neidhart and Keith, which led
into their tag match.

Halme's night didn't get better. Jim Neidhart and Keith Hart beat him and
Savio Vega when the Anvil flattened Halme with a flying shoulderblock.
Halme is now winless in five matches on the tour. After the match, Halmes
frustrations came to a head when he shoved Tiger Hattori to the floor on his 
way to the back!

Kim Duk is still perfect. He and Tiger Jeet Singh beat Shiro Koshinaka and
Kuniaki Kobayashi when Duk choked Kobayashi out with a sleeper at 14:30.
That's six wins in six nights for Duk.

Bam Bam Bigelow flattened Hiro Saito with Greetings from Asbury Park at
8:11.

In the main event Mutoh, Chono and Sasaki beat Riki Choshu, Tatsumi
Fujinami and Osamu Kido when Chono trapped Kido in the STF at 17:23. Kido
held on as long as any man could before tapping. Afterward Chono took the
microphone with Mutoh and Sasaki beside him and held up the STF sign for
the crowd: Kido tonight, Choshu at Sumo Hall.
