Match observations: 

THE SAMOANS LEAVE A MARK
Night 6 | Hiroshima, Sun Plaza Hall | 1991-07-14

Summer Crush '91 reached the Sun Plaza Hall in Hiroshima on Sunday night,
and the tour's sixth stop ended the way the last three have: with the
Samoans standing over a New Japan champion.

Riki Choshu came out for the main event without Shinya Hashimoto, who
wasn't in the building. Hiroyoshi Tenzan stepped in beside Choshu and Junji
Hirata against Samu, Kokina and Tarzan Goto. For fifteen minutes it was a
fight more than a match. The end came when Hirata had Samu reeling and Afa
came over the rail and hit him from behind. The referee threw it out and
gave the win to Choshu's team, but that didn't stop anybody. Samu and
Kokina put the boots to Hirata until the young lions dragged them away.

Choshu helped his tag champion to his feet and took the microphone. The
Samoans have been coming for him since Nagoya, he said, and now they've
come for Hirata. If they want the tag belts at Sumo Hall, they go through
him and the champions every night until July 30.

The match of the night came one bout earlier. Keiji Mutoh and Masahiro
Chono went eighteen minutes with Tatsumi Fujinami and Shiro Koshinaka
before Mutoh climbed to the top and finished Koshinaka with the moonsault.
Chono stood on the apron and pointed at the crowd: Sumo Hall is two weeks
away.

Pegasus Kid won his rematch with Osamu Kido, who beat him in Nagoya, with a
diving headbutt at 17:36. It cost him. Kid landed badly earlier in the
match and left holding his neck, and he's expected to miss time. His IWGP
Junior Heavyweight Title match with Jushin Liger at Sumo Hall is now in
question.

Bam Bam Bigelow pinned Keith Hart with the Greetings from Asbury Park, Mil
Mascaras splashed Norio Honaga to win the six-man for the lucha trio with
Dos Caras and Gran Hamada, and Hiro Saito and Kengo Kimura beat Tony Halme
and Jeff Gaylord when Saito suplexed Halme. That's four straight losses for
Halme on the tour, and the Hiroshima crowd let him hear it. El Samurai beat
Akira Nogami in the opener.

The tour moves to Fukuoka on Thursday.
