Osamu Kido is still on the mat, holding his neck, as the young lions help
him to his feet and walk him to the back. Masahiro Chono stays in the
ring. Keiji Mutoh stands beside him. Kensuke Sasaki, who walked into this
building as a replacement, stays in the corner behind them and does not
leave. A young lion hands Chono the microphone.

MASAHIRO CHONO: Fukuoka! Kido-san is a hard man. Seventeen minutes. Nobody
gets out of the STF, and he still did not want to give up. I respect that.

Chono raises his right hand and makes the STF sign, fingers bent like a
lock, and turns slowly so every side of the building can see it.

MASAHIRO CHONO: Tonight, Kido. In Sapporo, the cameras will be there. And
on July 30th at Sumo Hall, Choshu.

The crowd reacts, a mix of cheers and whistles.

MASAHIRO CHONO: Choshu, you called my name on the first night of this
tour. You told everyone you wanted me. You have the belt, you have the
lariat, you have all of New Japan behind you. I have this.

He holds up the STF sign again.

MASAHIRO CHONO: Ask Hase. Ask Halme. Ask Kido. Everybody I put in it says
the same thing. Sumo Hall, Choshu. The STF takes your belt.

Mutoh leans into the microphone.

KEIJI MUTOH: Chono takes Choshu. And Inoki-san, I have not forgotten
Niigata.

Chono turns and holds the microphone out toward Sasaki. Sasaki looks at
it, looks at the crowd, and says nothing. After a long moment he nods once
at Chono, steps through the ropes and walks to the back on his own. Chono
and Mutoh watch him go, then raise their hands together as the music hits.
