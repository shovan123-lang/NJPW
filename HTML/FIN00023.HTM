NJPW Summer Crush '91 Night 5 - Closing Promo: Choshu and the Champions
Abashiri, Abashiri City Gymnasium | Segment 8 (3:00)

The Samoans are still on the floor, shoving young lions out of the way and
jawing at the ring. Samu points up at Riki Choshu. Kokina slaps the apron
with both hands. Junji Hirata and Shinya Hashimoto stand at the ropes until
the two Samoans finally back up the aisle, and the Abashiri crowd lets them
hear it all the way to the curtain.

Choshu rolls his neck, takes a towel from a young lion and wipes his face.
The ring announcer hands him the microphone. He doesn't wait for the
building to quiet down.

RIKI CHOSHU: Abashiri! You came a long way tonight. So did we.

RIKI CHOSHU: Samoans. You come to New Japan. You put your hands on Hiro
Saito in Nagoya. You put your hands on me tonight. You think this is
America? You think you jump a man from behind and walk away laughing? You
picked the wrong fight. You picked the wrong company. And you picked the
wrong man.

He points the microphone up the aisle.

RIKI CHOSHU: I don't hide. Hiroshima tomorrow night. Fukuoka. Sapporo.
Sendai. Every night on this tour, you'll find me. You want Choshu? Come and
get Choshu.

He hands the microphone to Hashimoto, who has an IWGP Tag Team belt over
his shoulder. Hirata holds up the other one beside him.

SHINYA HASHIMOTO: Samu. Kokina. You want to make noise? Make it for these.

He slaps the belt.

SHINYA HASHIMOTO: Hirata and I are the IWGP Tag Team Champions. These belts
are waiting for you. Any night, any city. But when you step in the ring
with us, there's no running to the floor. I'll kick your heads off.

Hirata says nothing. He nods once and raises his belt higher. The crowd
roars for him.

Choshu takes the microphone back for the last word.

RIKI CHOSHU: And Chono. I saw you tonight. "Sumo Hall." I heard you. July
30th, you get your match. And you get my lariat. Abashiri, thank you!
Summer Crush goes on!

Choshu raises the IWGP Heavyweight Championship over his head. Hirata and
Hashimoto raise the tag belts on either side of him. The three of them
stand in the center of the ring as the music hits and the whole building
comes to its feet. The show closes on them.
