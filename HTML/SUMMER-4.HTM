Match observations: 

SUMMER CRUSH '91: CHOSHU TO THE SAMOANS: "YOU PICKED THE WRONG MAN"
Abashiri City Gymnasium, Hokkaido - July 13, 1991

New Japan's Summer Crush tour reached the far north on Saturday, and IWGP
Heavyweight Champion Riki Choshu sent a message across the whole country.

Three nights after the Samoans flattened him and Hiro Saito in under three
minutes in Nagoya, Choshu teamed with IWGP Tag Team Champions Junji Hirata
and Shinya Hashimoto against Samu, Kokina and Scott Norton. Afa, the
Samoans' patriarch, got involved and attacked Hirata, but the champion had
the last word. Choshu came off the ropes with a diving lariat on Norton for
the win.

Then, as New Japan tradition demands, the winners took the microphone.
Choshu told the Samoans they had picked the wrong fight, the wrong company
and the wrong man. Hashimoto went further. Holding up his title belt, he
told Samu and Kokina that the IWGP tag titles are waiting for them, any
night and any city.

The challenger was watching. Earlier in the night, Masahiro Chono teamed
with Keiji Mutoh to beat former champions Hiroshi Hase and Kensuke Sasaki,
forcing Hase to submit to the STF after 22 minutes. Chono looked into the
crowd and said two words: Sumo Hall. He gets his shot at Choshu's title on
July 30.

Elsewhere on the card, junior heavyweight challenger the Pegasus Kid beat
veteran Mando Guerrero with his diving headbutt. Kim Duk stayed unbeaten on
the tour with a win over Osamu Kido, and Tiger Jeet Singh punished Shiro
Koshinaka with the Tiger Claw after the bell. Tarzan Goto beat karate man
Masashi Aoyagi with a facebuster, and young lion Hiroyoshi Tenzan won the
opener over Savio Vega.

Summer Crush continues Sunday at Hiroshima's Sun Plaza Hall.
